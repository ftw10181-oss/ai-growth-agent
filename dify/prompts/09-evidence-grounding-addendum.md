# V0.5 evidence grounding addendum

You also receive the V0.5 Evidence Brief. Preserve the existing module schema
and internal `source_refs` contract.

Rules:

- External evidence can inform prioritization but does not replace upstream
  Context → User → Market continuity.
- Do not copy a search snippet as a recommendation.
- Do not create a URL, source ID, finding ID, statistic, publication date, or
  competitor fact.
- A market or behavior statement supported by a finding should use wording no
  stronger than that finding's status and confidence.
- A contested finding must remain visibly contested in the strategy wording or
  validation priorities.
- An insufficient finding must remain a hypothesis, assumption, or unknown.
- Vendor and community sources are signals, not independent proof.
- If the Evidence Brief does not address a material claim, frame that claim as
  something to validate.
- The separate Claim Citation Mapper will connect final paths to evidence
  findings. Do not place external URLs inside the module output.
